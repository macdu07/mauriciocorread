## Direction contract

THESIS: Each case is the home poster opened into a printed dossier: same plate colour, same poster name, same Imprenta screenshot, followed by a ticket-style technical sheet and plain, readable text. Refuses the generic case template of a hero image above a text column and a thumbnail grid.

OWN-WORLD: Inherits DESIGN.md: plate per project in the poster rotation (orange, turquoise, cream, gunmetal…); Archivo poster name fitted to its longest word (never broken mid-word); Geist reading text on a cream plate; compact Ticket for the sheet; gunmetal "What we built" plate with a tonal ghost word and numbered mono list; an alternate colour plate for desktop+phone views; gunmetal CTA; next-project plate in the next case's tone.

STORY: Recognise the project from the poster, read the context and challenge, scan what was built, see it on desktop and phone, contact or move to the next case.
