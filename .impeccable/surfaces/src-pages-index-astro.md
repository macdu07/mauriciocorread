## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a retro-futurist print edition coming alive: each section is a full-bleed poster plate, scroll turns the pages. Refuses the category default of split hero + rows of equal service cards on a neutral ground.

OWN-WORLD: Cream paper with fine grain against full-bleed turquoise, orange and gunmetal fields; giant condensed Archivo poster lettering that swaps colour where it crosses a colour field; Geist for everything read; the brand M as window, mask and portal; rotating circular stamps, ticket stubs, thin orbit linework, four-point stars, registration marks and folios in the margins (never as eyebrows above headings); duotone imagery that registers like a print run.

STORY: Visitor learns in one viewport who Mauricio is and what he does, sees real client sites floating inside his M, crosses through it into his point of view, then the work, the services, the process, and leaves through a boarding-ticket CTA to WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: thin nav row (M logo, 4 links, ES/EN, orange "Hablemos" button). Right ~41%: full-height turquoise field (turquoise→deep turquoise; gunmetal lives inside the M so the window keeps contrast) and a huge M window (~70vh, straddling the cream/turquoise seam) with real project screens in browser frames, orange sun disc and orbit lines, layered in parallax. Left: MAURICIO / CORREA in Archivo condensed black at ~16vw, gunmetal, running into the M and the turquoise field, where letters turn cream (decorative; H1 carries the accessible text). Under the name bottom-left: H1 "Transformo ideas en experiencias digitales." (Geist Bold), one support line, primary "Ver proyectos" (gunmetal) + "Hablemos" (orange). Rotating stamp "UI/UX · WEB · BRANDING · +10 AÑOS" on the seam. Mobile: M window on top, name stack, H1 and buttons.

Motion grammar: expo-out line/char reveals, print-registration image entrances, scroll-velocity width breathing on display type, stamp spin.
