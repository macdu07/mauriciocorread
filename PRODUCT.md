## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output) + TypeScript, GSAP 3 + Lenis for motion. No CMS for now; all editable content lives in Astro Content Layer collections with Zod schemas so a Strapi loader can replace the local loaders later without touching components. Deploy target: Coolify (Docker image: pnpm build + nginx), changed from Cloudflare Pages on 2026-10-21. Confirmed by Mauricio on 2026-10-20.

## Users

Business owners and independent professionals (Latin America and the US) who need a new website or want to improve the one they have. They arrive from Instagram, LinkedIn, referrals or a shared link, usually on a phone, and judge in seconds whether Mauricio can make their brand look serious and work well online. Hypothesis carried from the content brief; not yet validated with data.

## Product Purpose

Personal site and portfolio of **Mauricio Correa — UI/UX design, web development and applied AI**. It presents his four connected services (UI/UX experience design, website and store development, AI and automation, corporate identity), proves them with real case studies, and turns visits into conversations on WhatsApp or email. The site itself is the main portfolio piece. Success: contact clicks, time spent in case studies, Lighthouse ≥ 90 across the board.

Copy is tool-agnostic on purpose: it sells outcomes (clear, fast, converting, less manual work), never a specific stack, because Mauricio picks the technology per project. ## Product Principles

1. The work speaks first: real cases, concrete decisions, live sites.
2. Clarity over spectacle: every effect serves reading order and the next action.
3. The site is proof: craft, speed and accessibility are part of the pitch.
4. One voice end to end: identity, interface and code presented as one practice.
