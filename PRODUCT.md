## Platform

## Stack

Astro (static output) + TypeScript, GSAP 3 + Lenis for motion. No CMS for now; all editable content lives in Astro Content Layer collections with Zod schemas so a Strapi loader can replace the local loaders later without touching components. Deploy target: Cloudflare Pages. Confirmed by Mauricio on 2026-10-20.

## Users

Business owners and independent professionals (Latin America and the US) who need a new website or want to improve the one they have. They arrive from Instagram, LinkedIn, referrals or a shared link, usually on a phone, and judge in seconds whether Mauricio can make their brand look serious and work well online. Hypothesis carried from the content brief; not yet validated with data.

Personal site and portfolio of **Mauricio Correa — UI/UX Web Designer & Web Developer**. ## Product Principles

1. The work speaks first: real cases, concrete decisions, live sites.
